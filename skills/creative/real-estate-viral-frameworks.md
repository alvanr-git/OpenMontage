## 2. Selectable Creative Frameworks (Pick What Fits the Brief)

When planning a script or scene plan, select the option that best matches the user's goal—or adapt freely:

### Option A: 6-Step Viral Reel Framework (High-Retention Short-Form)
Best for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts targeting broad discovery and lead capture (`35–60s`, `vertical_9_16`):
1. **0–3s Scroll-Stopping Hook**: Bold visual + curiosity or contrarian data statement (e.g., *"Why this $5.8M semi-D is actually priced at land value..."*).
2. **3–8s Visual Bridge**: Fast contextual transition establishing location, district, or market anomaly.
3. **8–20s Friend-POV Storytelling**: Conversational, jargon-free explanation as if walking a close friend through the numbers on site.
4. **20–35s Asset / Project Reveal**: Crisp floorplan, site boundary, or architectural elevation overlay highlighting the core thesis.
5. **35–50s PPP Selling Pointers**:
   - **Price**: Entry PSF vs. surrounding comparables or replacement cost.
   - **Product**: Layout efficiency, GFA optimization, or plot geometry.
   - **Potential**: URA Master Plan catalyst, exit buyer pool, or rental yield floor.
6. **50–60s 5-Part Lead Magnet CTA**: Specific keyword trigger + clear deliverable (e.g., *"Comment **LANDED** and I'll DM you the full 5-plot district comparison sheet"*).

### Option B: Architectural & Landed Subdivision Breakdown
Best for deep-dive analytical explainers (usable by either `alvanassets` or `propertyspy`):
- **Subdivision & Plot Geometry**: Highlight SLA/URA minimum plot size rules (e.g., `200 sqm` minimum for Semi-D, `8m` minimum frontage width) using clean vector boundary lines.
- **A&A vs. Rebuild Economics**: Contrast Addition & Alteration (`$350–$550 psf` construction cost, preserving existing envelope) against full teardown and rebuild (`$550–$750+ psf`).
- **Hidden Due-Diligence Checks**: Visualize LTA Road Line Plan reserves, PUB sewer easements, and GFA void minimization so viewers see tangible technical alpha.

### Option C: Commercial & Co-Living Yield Arbitrage
Best for investor-focused content comparing asset classes:
- **The Yield Spread**: Contrast typical `2.5%–3.2%` gross residential condo yields (plus 20%–30% ABSD for multi-property owners) against `6.5%–8.8%` net yields on commercial shophouses, co-living hostels, or worker dormitories with `0%` ABSD.
- **Cashflow Waterfall Visuals**: Use clean animated stat cards and bar comparisons (`stat_reveal`, `chart_comparison`) to show monthly net cashflow after financing.

### Option D: New Launch & Exclusive Listing Spotlight
Best for showcasing any of the 174+ new launches or `ListingsWhale` active properties:
- Pull renders, unit mixes, and floorplans from `new_launch_media` (`D01`–`D28`) and the 3-paragraph dossier from `listings_whale_master`.
- Structure around **3 Buyer Questions**:
  1. *Who is the exact exit buyer in 5–7 years?*
  2. *Which stack/layout avoids afternoon west sun and highway noise?*
  3. *How does the entry PSF compare against adjacent resale condos/landed plots?*

### Option E: Market News Reaction / Negotiation Story / Freeform
- **Rapid News Breakdown**: Pair headlines from `news_archive` with historical URA transaction charts to explain what a policy change or GLS land bid actually means for buyers on the ground.
- **Top-Scorer Negotiation Story**: Narrative case study of how a deal was structured, valued, or rescued.
- **Freeform / Reference-Driven**: Ignore preset structures entirely and follow the user's custom brief or reference video.

Two optional Singapore real-estate playbooks are available in `styles/`:

- **Any Other Theme**: You can also use `premium-minimalist`, `clean-professional`, `flat-motion-graphics`, `minimalist-diagram`, or a custom Atelier design.

**Optional Visual & Pacing Tips (from `alvanassets` & `SocialMediaAgent` learnings):**
- **One Core Idea Per Scene**: Keep mobile vertical frames (`9:16`) uncluttered with ~20% margin breathing room around stat cards and floorplan callouts.
- **A/B Hook Variants**: When generating scripts or proposals, consider offering two hook angles modeled on `SocialMediaAgent`:
  - *Variant A (Urgency / Contrarian Anomaly)*: Challenges a common market assumption in the first 3 seconds.
  - *Variant B (Authority / Analytical Blueprint)*: Leads with a concrete dataset or architectural metric.
- **Photography & B-Roll Feel**: When generating AI B-roll or grading stills, warm natural daylight (`35mm` architectural lens feel, honest materials like travertine, timber, and tropical greenery) pairs naturally with both brand themes.
